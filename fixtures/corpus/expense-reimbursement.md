# Expense Reimbursement

Employees are reimbursed for reasonable business expenses when they follow these rules.

## What is covered

Travel booked through the company portal, meals during business trips, client entertainment and work-related software are covered. Commuting costs, alcohol with personal meals and fines are not covered.

## Spending limits

Meals are limited to 60 dollars per person per day while travelling. Hotels must be booked below the city rate published in the travel portal. Flights longer than six hours may be booked in premium economy. Any single expense above 500 dollars needs written approval from a manager before the purchase.

## Submitting expenses

Submit expenses in the expense tool within 30 days of the purchase and attach an itemised receipt. Card slips without an itemised receipt are rejected. Reports are approved by the manager and finance reviews them weekly.

## Payment

Approved expenses are paid with the next payroll run. Employees who hold a company card do not need reimbursement but must still submit receipts for every transaction by the 5th of the following month.
