# Data Retention Policy

We keep customer data only as long as it is needed to provide the service or to meet legal obligations.

## Retention periods

Application logs are kept for 30 days. Audit logs that record administrative actions are kept for 13 months. Invoices and tax records are kept for 7 years as required by law. Support tickets are deleted 24 months after they are closed.

## Account deletion

When a customer closes their account, all content is soft deleted immediately and permanently erased after 30 days. During this grace period the customer can ask support to restore the account. Backups containing the deleted data expire on their normal schedule and are never restored into production afterwards.

## Data export

Customers can export their projects as a ZIP archive containing JSON and the original uploaded files. Exports are prepared in the background and the download link is emailed when ready. The link is valid for 72 hours.

## Deletion requests

Individual users can ask for their personal data to be deleted by writing to the privacy mailbox. We confirm the request within 7 days and complete it within 30 days.
