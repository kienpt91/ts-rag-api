# Incident Severity Levels

Severity decides who is paged, how quickly we respond and how we communicate with customers.

## SEV1 - critical

The product is down for most customers or customer data is at risk. Response is immediate and an incident commander is appointed. Status page updates are published every 30 minutes until resolution. A written postmortem is required within five business days.

## SEV2 - major

A core feature is degraded or unavailable for a subset of customers, with no workaround. The on-call engineer responds within 15 minutes. Status page updates are published every hour.

## SEV3 - minor

A non-critical feature is impaired or a workaround exists. Work is handled during business hours and tracked in the normal ticket queue. No status page update is needed unless customers ask.

## Postmortems

Postmortems are blameless. They describe the timeline, the contributing factors and the follow-up actions with owners and due dates. Action items from SEV1 postmortems are reviewed in the weekly operations meeting until they are closed.
