# Refund Policy

This policy explains when customers can get their money back for subscriptions and one-off purchases.

## Eligibility

Customers may request a refund within 30 days of the original purchase date. Annual plans are refunded in full during this window. Monthly plans are refunded for the current billing month only. Requests made after 30 days are reviewed case by case and are approved only when the service was unavailable for more than 48 hours.

## How to request a refund

Open a ticket from the billing page and choose the category "Refund request". Include the invoice number and a short reason. Support replies within one business day. Refunds cannot be requested by phone or by social media message.

## Processing time

Approved refunds are sent back to the original payment method. Card refunds usually appear within 5 to 10 business days, depending on the issuing bank. Bank transfers take up to 14 days. Store credit is applied immediately and never expires.

## Non-refundable items

Domain registrations, custom development hours and gift cards are not refundable once delivered. Usage-based overage charges are refundable only if they were caused by a billing error on our side.
