# Password and Access Policy

Every employee account must follow these rules to keep company systems secure.

## Password requirements

Passwords must contain at least 14 characters and must not appear in the list of commonly breached passwords. Password managers are provided to all staff and are the only approved place to store credentials. Passwords are rotated only after a suspected compromise, not on a calendar schedule.

## Multi-factor authentication

Hardware security keys are required for production access, the source code host and the cloud console. Authenticator apps are accepted for all other systems. SMS codes are not allowed as a second factor. Lost keys must be reported to the security team within one hour so that they can be revoked.

## Resetting a forgotten password

Use the self-service reset page and confirm your identity with your second factor. If you no longer have your second factor, ask your manager to file an identity verification request with the IT desk. The IT desk will verify you on a video call before issuing a temporary credential that expires after 24 hours.

## Offboarding

Access is removed on the employee's last day at 17:00 local time. Shared secrets that the person could read are rotated within five business days.
