# On-Call Rotation

The platform team shares an on-call rotation so that someone can always respond to production alerts.

## Schedule

Each shift lasts one week and starts on Monday at 09:00 UTC. Two engineers are on call at once: a primary who receives pages first and a secondary who is paged if the primary does not acknowledge within 10 minutes. Swaps are arranged in the team channel and recorded in the scheduling tool at least 24 hours ahead.

## Responsibilities

The primary acknowledges pages, triages the alert and either fixes the problem or escalates it. Between pages, on-call engineers work on reliability tasks from the backlog rather than feature work. A handoff note summarising open issues is posted every Monday morning.

## Compensation

On-call engineers receive a flat weekly stipend plus paid time off equal to the hours worked outside normal working hours. Pages that last longer than two hours qualify for a full day off the following week.

## Escalation

If neither engineer responds within 20 minutes, the pager escalates to the engineering manager and then to the head of infrastructure.
